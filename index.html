Key Takeaways

  • 📋893 students analyzed across grades 2–8: 449 boys and 444 girls.
  • 📖STAR Reading: Boys grew +4.1 points year over year. Girls grew +0.7 points.
  • 🔢STAR Math: Boys changed -1.5 points year over year. Girls changed -0.7 points.
  • 📝CAASPP: Girls led in ELA proficiency (38.7% vs 34.8%). Boys led in Math (30.5% vs 23.0%).
  • ⚠️Discipline gap: Boys averaged 1.1 incidents vs 0.3 for girls. 26.8% of boys had at least one incident compared to 11.5% of girls.
  • 🏫Attendance was similar: Boys 93.9% vs Girls 94.1%. Chronic absenteeism: Boys 15.3% vs Girls 14.0%.
  • 🟢Biggest improvement: The STAR Reading (Mean) gender gap narrowed from 4.7 to 1.3 pts.

Data notes: Attendance, discipline, and course grades are available as a single snapshot (not split by year in the source data). STAR scores span 6 testing windows across both school years, so year-over-year STAR comparisons are available. CAASPP scores are from 2024-25 only. Course grades are secondary (grades 6-8) only. Chronic absenteeism = below 90% attendance rate. "Pass" = grades A through D (including +/−) or P. "Not pass" = F. Grades of CR and NM are excluded.
